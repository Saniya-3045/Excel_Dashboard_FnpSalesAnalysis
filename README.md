# 📊 FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data from FNP to understand revenue trends, customer spending, product performance, occasions, city-wise orders, and delivery performance.

An interactive Excel dashboard was created to transform the raw sales data into meaningful business insights. The dashboard uses Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Timeline Filters to provide an easy-to-understand view of sales performance.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze FNP sales data and identify key business trends that can help improve sales performance and customer satisfaction.

The analysis focuses on:

- Overall revenue performance
- Customer spending behavior
- Delivery performance
- Monthly sales trends
- Product performance
- City-wise order volume
- Occasion-wise revenue
- Category-wise revenue
- Hourly revenue patterns

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Total Revenue
Analyzed the overall revenue generated from FNP sales.

**Total Revenue: ₹3.35 Crore**

### 2. Customer Spending Analysis
Calculated the average amount spent by customers to understand customer spending behavior.

**Average Customer Spend: ₹3,520**

### 3. Delivery Performance
Analyzed the average number of days taken to deliver orders.

**Average Delivery Time: 5.53 Days**

### 4. Monthly Sales Performance
Analyzed revenue across different months to identify seasonal sales trends and high-performing periods.

### 5. Top 5 Products by Revenue
Identified the top-performing products based on revenue generated.

This helps understand which products contribute significantly to overall sales.

### 6. Top 10 Cities by Number of Orders
Analyzed order volume across cities to identify locations with the highest number of orders.

### 7. Revenue by Occasion
Compared revenue generated across different occasions, including:

- Anniversary
- Birthday
- Diwali
- Holi
- Raksha Bandhan
- Valentine's Day
- Other Occasions

### 8. Revenue by Category
Compared revenue across different product categories such as:

- Cakes
- Colors
- Mugs
- Plants
- Raksha Bandhan
- Soft Toys
- Sweets

### 9. Revenue by Hour
Analyzed revenue generated at different hours of the day to understand customer ordering patterns and identify high-performing time periods.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- FNP generated approximately **₹3.35 Crore in total revenue**.
- The average customer spending was approximately **₹3,520**.
- The average delivery time was approximately **5.53 days**.
- Sales vary across months, indicating seasonal patterns in customer demand.
- A small group of top-performing products contributes significantly to overall revenue.
- Order volumes vary across cities, helping identify locations with stronger customer demand.
- Revenue differs across occasions, highlighting important seasonal and celebration-based sales opportunities.
- Product categories show different levels of revenue contribution.
- Hour-wise revenue analysis helps identify periods of higher customer purchasing activity.
